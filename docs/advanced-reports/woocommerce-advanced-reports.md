# WooCommerce Advanced Reports

FluentCRM provides an advanced reporting suite for WooCommerce, allowing you to bridge the gap between your store's sales performance and your marketing efforts. This dashboard offers a deep dive into revenue trends, customer growth, and product performance.

To access these reports, navigate to the **Reports** dropdown in the top navbar and select **WooCommerce** from the left sidebar.


## 1. Sales Performance Overview

The top section features six key metric cards that provide an instant snapshot of your store’s health:

* **Total Revenue:** The aggregate income generated from all successful orders.
* **Total Orders:** The total number of transactions processed.
* **Total Customers:** The number of unique individuals who have purchased from your store.
* **Average Revenue Per Customer:** The average lifetime value (LTV) of your buyers.
* **Average Order Value (AOV):** The average amount spent per transaction.
* **Average Orders Per Customer (AOC):** A metric showing how frequently your customers return to shop.

## 2. Visual Growth Charts

This interactive section allows you to visualize two major aspects of your business using line and area graphs:

* **Gross Volume:** Tracks your daily revenue performance.
* **Customer Growth:** Monitors how quickly you are acquiring new buyers.
* **Date & Product Filtering:** You can filter the data by a specific date range or isolate the performance of specific products.
* **Comparison Feature:** Compare the **Current Range** (Blue) against a **Compare Range** (Green) from the previous month or year to measure growth trends.

## 3. Detailed Comparison Table

For a more granular audit, the **Comparison Table** breaks down the data day-by-day.

* **Range Contrast:** View the exact values for the current period versus the comparison period.
* **Percentage Change:** See the calculation of growth or decline for each day.
* **Data Export:** Use the **Export CSV** button to download this data for external analysis or client reporting.

### 4. Top Selling Products

The bottom section identifies your most popular items based on revenue and quantity sold.

* **Product Insights:** View the specific product names (e.g., *Hoodie*), the total revenue they generated, and the number of times they were purchased.
* **Performance Context:** This helps you identify which items should be featured in your next email campaign or sequence.
