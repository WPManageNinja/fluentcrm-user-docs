# Abandoned Cart Report

The [Abandoned Cart](https://fluentcrm.com/what-is-abandoned-cart/) report in FluentCRM provides a detailed overview of lost sales opportunities, recovered revenue, and customer behavior. Currently, this reporting feature focuses exclusively on WooCommerce cart data.

## Analytics Overview

At the top of the report, you will find a visual graph showing your cart activity over time. Use the **Date Filter** in the top right corner to refine the statistics for specific time frames, such as the last 7 days, 30 days, or a custom range.

The dashboard displays three key metrics:

 * **Abandoned Cart:** The total number of carts that were started but not completed.
 * **Recoverable Revenue:** The potential value of all currently abandoned carts.
 * **Recovered Revenue:** The actual revenue earned from carts that were successfully recovered through your automations.

## Customer Details & Cart Insights

Scroll down to the **Abandoned Cart Details** section to see a list of individual customers who left items in their carts. This table provides the customer's name, email, the date the cart was abandoned, and the total value of the items.

To get a deeper look at what a specific customer intended to buy:

Locate the customer in the list. Go to **three-dot** icon and click the **View Cart** button on the right side.

A popup will appear showing the specific products left in the cart, their quantity, and the individual prices.
