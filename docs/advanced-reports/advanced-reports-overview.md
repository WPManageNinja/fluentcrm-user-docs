# Advanced Reports

FluentCRM's Reports dashboard gives you a unified view of your contacts, email performance, and the revenue impact of your marketing — all without leaving WordPress. Reports pull live data from FluentCRM and any connected plugins (WooCommerce, EDD, LearnDash, LifterLMS) so you always see an up-to-date picture.

To access reports, click the **Reports** dropdown in the top navigation bar of your FluentCRM dashboard.

## Available Reports

### Contact & Email Reports

These reports are available to all FluentCRM users and cover your core CRM and email activity.

| Report | What it shows |
|--------|--------------|
| [CRM Contact Report](/crm-contact-report) | Contact database health — status breakdown, geographic distribution, growth over time, and top lists and tags |
| [Emails Report](/emails-report) | Email marketing performance — sent, delivered, opened, clicked, and bounced metrics across all campaigns and automations |
| [Abandoned Cart Report](/abandoned-cart-report) | Revenue lost, recovered, and in-progress from your abandoned cart recovery funnels |

### E-commerce Reports

These reports appear when the corresponding plugin is installed and synced with FluentCRM.

| Report | What it shows |
|--------|--------------|
| [WooCommerce Advanced Reports](/woocommerce-advanced-reports) | Sales performance, revenue trends, customer growth, and top-selling products from your WooCommerce store |
| [EDD Advanced Reports](/edd-advanced-reports) | Digital product sales, customer acquisition, and product performance from Easy Digital Downloads |

### LMS Reports

These reports appear when the corresponding LMS plugin is installed and synced with FluentCRM.

| Report | What it shows |
|--------|--------------|
| [LearnDash Advanced Reports](/learndash-advanced-reports) | Course enrollment trends, student growth, and course performance data from LearnDash |
| [LifterLMS Advanced Reports](/lifterlms-advanced-reports) | Course and membership enrollment trends and student metrics from LifterLMS |

>[!Note]
> E-commerce and LMS reports require the respective plugin to be installed, activated, and synced with FluentCRM. If a report tab does not appear in your dashboard, check that the integration is active under **FluentCRM → Settings → Integrations**.
