# The FluentCRM Dashboard

The FluentCRM Dashboard serves as the central command center for your email marketing and customer relationship management activities. It provides a real-time, high-level overview of your marketing health, subscriber growth, and campaign performance.

## Overview of the FluentCRM Dashboard

The dashboard is designed to provide actionable insights at a glance, allowing you to monitor the most critical aspects of your marketing funnels without digging through complex menus.

### 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
At the very top, you’ll find four primary stat cards that summarize your current standing:
* **Active Contacts:** The total number of unique, subscribed individuals currently in your database.
* **Campaigns:** The total number of broadcast email campaigns you have created.
* **Emails Sent:** The cumulative number of individual emails delivered to your contacts.
* **Active Automations:** The number of automated marketing funnels currently "Live" and processing contacts.

### 2. Subscribers Growth Chart
This interactive visual graph tracks your audience's expansion over time.
* **Toggle Views:** You can switch between "By Date" (showing daily sign-up spikes) and "Cumulative" (showing the steady total growth of your list).
* **Date Range Selector:** Customize the timeframe to analyze growth over specific months or years.
* **Data Points:** Hover over the orange (daily) or purple (cumulative) lines to see exact numbers for a specific date.

### 3. Email Performance (Sidebar)
This section provides a visual breakdown of your email deliverability and engagement over a set period (e.g., the last 90 days).
* **Sent & Delivered:** Tracks the percentage of emails successfully reaching the recipient's server.
* **Opened:** Displays your average open rate, a key indicator of subject line effectiveness.
* **Clicked:** Shows the percentage of users taking action on your call-to-action (CTA) buttons or links.
* **Bounced:** Monitors failed deliveries, helping you maintain a "healthy" sender reputation.

## Recent Activity Sections

The bottom half of the dashboard keeps you updated on your most recent marketing movements.

### Recent Campaigns
See your latest broadcast emails along with their immediate results. Each entry shows the number of recipients, the delivery status, and the current Open Rate. You can click **View All** to manage your full campaign history.

### Recent Contacts
A quick-view list of the latest people to join your list. It shows their name, email address, and current status (e.g., Subscribed).

### Active Automations
This list highlights your top-running funnels, such as *Abandon Cart Automations* or *Joined In Any Space* triggers. It serves as a shortcut to jump back into your automation builder to make tweaks.

## Quick Links & Resources

Located on the right sidebar, the Quick Links section provides one-click access to essential administrative tasks:
* **Contact Segments:** Jump straight to managing your Tags and Lists.
* **Email Sequences:** Access your automated drip campaigns.
* **Documentations & Video Tutorials:** Direct links to help guides if you get stuck.
* **SMTP/Mail Settings:** Quickly verify your email sending connection to ensure maximum deliverability.
