# FluentCRM Integrations

FluentCRM connects with the most popular WordPress plugins across e-commerce, LMS, membership, community, and page-builder categories. Each integration lets you automatically create or update contacts, segment your audience, and trigger marketing automations — all from within your WordPress dashboard.

>[!Note]
> Some integrations require **FluentCRM Pro**. [See what's included →](/how-to-install-upgrade-and-activate-license)

## Forms & E-commerce

These integrations cover your sales channels and lead capture forms.

| Integration | What it does |
|-------------|-------------|
| [WP Fluent Forms](/wp-fluent-forms-integration-with-fluentcrm) | Add contacts to FluentCRM from any Fluent Forms submission and map form fields to CRM contact properties |
| [WooCommerce](/woocommerce-automation-with-fluentcrm) | Sync customers, trigger automations on order events, and segment buyers by product or purchase history |
| [FluentCart](/fluentcart-integration-with-fluentcrm) | Automate post-purchase flows and subscription lifecycle events directly from FluentCart orders |
| [SureCart](/surecart-automation-with-fluentcrm) | Trigger automations when SureCart orders succeed or are revoked |
| [Easy Digital Downloads](/easy-digital-downloads-integration-with-fluentcrm) | Sync EDD customers and fire automations on purchases, refunds, renewals, and license events |

## LMS Integrations

Connect FluentCRM to your learning platform to enroll students, track course progress, and automate follow-ups.

| Integration | What it does |
|-------------|-------------|
| [LearnDash](/learndash-integration-with-fluentcrm) | Trigger automations on course enrollment, completion, and group membership events |
| [LifterLMS](/lifterlms-integration-with-fluentcrm) | React to course and membership enrollments with automated email sequences |
| [LearnPress](/learpress-integration-with-fluentcrm) | Sync LearnPress students and trigger automations based on course activity |
| [Tutor LMS](/tutorlms-integration-with-fluentcrm) | Automate contact creation and tagging based on Tutor LMS course enrollments |
